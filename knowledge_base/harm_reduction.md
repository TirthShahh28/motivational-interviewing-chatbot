# Harm Reduction Principles for Alcohol

## What is Harm Reduction?

Harm reduction is a set of practical strategies that aim to reduce the negative consequences of alcohol use. It meets people where they are without requiring abstinence as a prerequisite.

Core principles:

- Pragmatic acceptance that alcohol use exists
- Focus on reducing harm rather than eliminating use
- Non-judgmental engagement
- Person-centered approach
- Recognition that any positive change matters

## Strategies to Discuss (When Appropriate)

### Pacing and Moderation

- Alternating alcoholic drinks with water
- Setting a limit before going out
- Eating before and while drinking
- Choosing lower-alcohol options
- Tracking drinks with an app

### Safety Strategies

- Never drinking and driving
- Having a designated driver or rideshare plan
- Staying with trusted friends
- Keeping phones charged
- Avoiding mixing alcohol with medications

### Understanding Patterns

- Noticing triggers (stress, social situations, boredom)
- Identifying high-risk times
- Recognizing escalation patterns
- Understanding the role alcohol plays in their life

## Important Boundaries

### We DO NOT:

- Tell people to stop drinking
- Make them feel judged for their choices
- Provide medical advice about withdrawal
- Diagnose alcohol use disorder
- Recommend specific medication or treatment plans

### We DO:

- Explore their relationship with alcohol
- Support their autonomy in decision-making
- Provide information when asked
- Encourage professional consultation when appropriate
- Validate their experiences

## When to Encourage Professional Help

Without being prescriptive, gently suggest professional support when:

- They express concern about physical symptoms
- They mention failed attempts to cut down
- They describe withdrawal symptoms
- They're drinking to manage mental health
- They express readiness for more structured support

Example language:

- "Have you ever talked to a doctor about what you've noticed?"
- "It might be helpful to get a professional perspective on this."
- "There are people who specialize in exactly what you're describing."
